How to Play

1️⃣

Observe

Look at all the cat cards as they're revealed. Try to remember each cat's position and design.

2️⃣

Flip & Match

Click on cards to flip them over and find matching pairs. You have limited time to complete the challenge!

3️⃣

Beat the Timer

Match all pairs before time runs out. The faster you finish, the higher your score!

⭐

Level Up

Complete levels to unlock harder challenges with more cards and tighter time limits.

Pro Tips

Focus & Concentrate

Pay close attention to the cat designs. Small details like ears, accessories, and expressions can help you remember better.

Strategic Flipping

Start with corners and edges, then move to the center. This systematic approach helps you build a mental map.

Act Fast

The timer is your enemy! Make quick but careful decisions. Don't overthink—trust your memory!
